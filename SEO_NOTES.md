# HeyDock search implementation and launch

39 pages with original service content, explicit definitions, practical scope, FAQs, internal links and implementation guidance. The content map covers managed IT, onsite dispatch, remote staffing, Microsoft 365, networks, backup, cybersecurity, data centres, rollouts, break/fix, IT asset disposal, AV, connectivity, consulting, business software and AI automation. Keywords are intent-led editorial choices, not a paid search-volume study.

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Python build.py plus enrich.py generates pages. content/catalog.json is the editable software catalogue. ChatGPT Sites hosts dist. No framework, database or CMS. The enquiry builder creates a local download; it does not send leads.

## Search metadata
Page titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, XML sitemap, Organization/WebSite/WebPage/BreadcrumbList structured data, Service markup on service pages and FAQPage markup matching visible answers. Structured data is not a promise of rich results or AI citations.

## Public launch
The current owner-private preview deliberately retains noindex/nofollow and robots Disallow. It cannot be marketed as an indexed public site. Before public launch, confirm brand/domain, company registration disclosures, real contact channels, receiving system, delivery scope and legal documents. Configure HEYDOCK_ORIGIN to the final HTTPS domain and HEYDOCK_PUBLIC=1 when rebuilding for an authorised public launch. Update hosting access separately; the build flag does not change audience. Verify final canonicals/robots/sitemap and submit the sitemap to verified Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools properties.

Publish genuine service evidence, named expertise and approved case studies as available. Maintain accurate profiles and software details. Avoid invented offices, reviews, certifications, response guarantees and mass-produced location pages. AI discovery depends on crawl access, useful original information and third-party evidence as well as on-page content; inclusion cannot be guaranteed. No special llms.txt or AI schema is required by Google.

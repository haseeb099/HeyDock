## Move workloads in waves, and leave some of them alone

A cloud migration is a programme of moves, tests and decisions about what not to touch yet. HeyDock plans and delivers those programmes for UK organisations, toward AWS, Azure or Google Cloud, or between them. We start with readiness: applications, data, identity, network, and the people who currently keep the lights on.

'Lift and shift everything this quarter' is a wish, not a plan. Some systems should be re-architected, some can move as they are, and some should stay until a dependency is retired. Saying that early is cheaper than saying it in a failed window.

## Who this is for

Teams leaving a data centre, leaving a landlord platform, or under pressure to 'be in the cloud' without a map. If you only need direction, use cloud solutions. If you have already moved and need an operator, use cloud management. Rollouts cover device and office moves; this page is about workloads and data.

## What we deliver

A readiness review, a per-workload approach, a wave plan, delivery, and a handover into operations. Critical systems need a rollback idea, even if rollback is unattractive. We will not guarantee zero downtime.

Poor documentation does not stop the work; it becomes the first phase.

## How the work runs

Ready, plan, move, hand over. Each wave has an owner, a test, and a communication plan for the people who will feel it. After the last agreed wave, cost and operations need a home, often in FinOps and cloud management.

Use this page when the essential job is to move agreed systems, on purpose, in an order you can defend.

## Fix the broken device, or replace it on purpose

Break-fix is the unglamorous work of a laptop that no longer charges, a desktop that will not post, a dock that has killed a row of screens, or a handset that failed overnight. HeyDock diagnoses those faults for UK businesses and coordinates an approved repair or replacement. The aim is a clear decision, not a week of guesswork while someone works from their phone.

We will not quietly 'have a go' at a device under someone else's warranty, and we will not treat a replacement as inevitable if a small part would do.

## Who this is for

Offices with a drawer of almost-working machines, IT teams who want a diagnosis before spending, and MSPs who need a local pair of hands. If the real need is a whole refresh, see rollouts. If the device is at end of life, see asset disposal. If the issue might be an account or an application, start with IT support so we do not dismantle a healthy machine.

## What we deliver

Diagnosis, a recommended action, and then only the work you approve. We will use your warranty or supplier where that is the sensible path. On-site, collection, or a vendor repair are all possible depending on the device and the site. You get a short record of the fault and the outcome.

Parts availability is not something we can invent. We will tell you if a part is slow or if replacement is cleaner.

## How the work runs

Describe, diagnose, propose, complete. Approval sits in the middle on purpose, so spend remains yours. Dispatch rules still apply for visits: access and a site contact confirmed first.

This service sits with engineer dispatch, IT support, rollouts and asset disposal. Use it when the core question is 'what is wrong with this piece of hardware, and what should we do with it?'

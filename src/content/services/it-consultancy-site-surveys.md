## Walk the building before you promise a plan

A site survey is how a drawing meets the cupboard under the stairs, the landlord’s rules, the already-full patch panel and the meeting room with no usable network point. HeyDock provides on-site IT surveys and the consultancy that follows, for UK workplaces. We write down what is actually there, including what we could not see.

A floor plan is not a survey. A quote written without a visit is a guess, and we would rather say that than decorate it.

## Who this is for

Teams planning a rollout or a refresh in an unfamiliar building, companies taking a new lease, and MSPs who want a pair of eyes before they quote. Wireless, rollouts, network support and dispatch often follow. A detailed radio survey, if needed, is called out rather than faked from a walk-through.

## What we deliver

A brief, a visit, a written picture, and recommendations that respect the building. Photographs only where permitted. Uncertainties are listed, because ceiling voids and landlord risers are often closed on the day.

## How the work runs

Brief, visit, write, next. Access is confirmed like any other attendance. The document should be something you can hand to a vendor without translation.

Use this page when the essential need is to see the site, honestly, before a plan is treated as firm.

## Practical limits and how to start

Walk the site, write down what is actually there, and turn that into a practical plan before a rollout, a network change or a move. A typical engagement begins with an arranged visit with access and a site contact, and then a walk-through of comms rooms, cabling routes, wireless concerns and workplace kit. It is usually a good fit for teams planning a rollout or a network refresh in a building they do not know well. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. A general site survey can include wireless observations. A detailed radio survey is part of the Wi-Fi service if you need that depth. Only if access can be arranged with the landlord or outgoing tenant. We will not invent a report from a floor plan alone and call it a survey. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

HeyDock is the customer-facing brand of Infintree Limited. Work is planned in English unless we have agreed otherwise. On-site attendance, where it applies, is confirmed per location rather than assumed from a coverage map.

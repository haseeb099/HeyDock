## Ask every vendor the same questions, and write down why you chose

Vendor selection goes wrong when the most charismatic demonstration wins, or when requirements are invented after the favourite has already been picked. HeyDock helps UK organisations evaluate technology vendors against a written brief, with a consistent set of questions and a recommendation that includes the doubts.

We work for you, not for the vendor. If we cannot be independent, we will not take the work.

## Who this is for

Teams lost in demos, organisations running a request for proposals, and leaders who will have to explain a purchase later. Business software and custom builds are two possible outcomes; sometimes the outcome is to buy nothing.

## What we deliver

A brief, a list, a fair evaluation, a recommendation, and better questions for the contract stage. We will not hide a weakness in the preferred option.

## How the work runs

Brief, list, evaluate, recommend. You remain the buyer. Implementation, if it follows, is a new brief.

Use this page when the essential need is a defensible choice, not another demonstration.

## Practical limits and how to start

Compare vendors against a written brief, with the same questions asked of each, and a recommendation you can defend. A typical engagement begins with a written requirements brief and must-haves, and then a longlist or shortlist with reasons for inclusion. It is usually a good fit for teams mid-way through a confusing set of vendor demos. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. No. If a referral arrangement ever existed it would be disclosed. The default is that we work for you. We can support a proportionate process. Public-sector procurement rules, if they apply, remain your responsibility with your advisers. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

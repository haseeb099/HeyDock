## Treat a rollout as a repeatable piece of work, not a heroic weekend

A rollout or an office migration goes well when the build is repeatable, the waves are honest, and someone still answers the phone on day two. HeyDock plans and delivers IT rollouts and workplace moves for UK organisations: laptops, phones, accessories, and the site work that makes a new floor usable. We would rather run a small pilot than discover a broken image on Monday morning with two hundred people waiting.

Logistics matter as much as the technical build. Access to the site, parking, a room to work in, and a named client contact are part of the plan, not footnotes.

## Who this is for

Companies replacing a fleet, teams moving office, and MSPs delivering a rollout for a client, including white-label where agreed. If you only need one broken device replaced, use break/fix. If you need a survey of the new site, consultancy and site surveys may come first.

## What we deliver

A written plan, a pilot, deployment in waves, and handover with a way to raise early issues. Imaging or configuration methods are agreed. Old devices are not ignored; disposal or reuse is planned, often with the asset disposal service.

We will not promise a one-night cutover if the numbers say otherwise. We will not treat furniture or construction as our scope.

## How the work runs

Plan, pilot, deploy, handover. Each wave has a confirmed site and a confirmed build. If the pilot fails, we stop and fix the cause. Engineer dispatch is often how people appear on site.

Use this page when many users or a site change need a coordinated, repeatable approach rather than a series of one-off visits.

## Practical limits and how to start

Pilot, deploy and hand over devices or office moves across agreed sites, with a repeatable build rather than a one-off scramble. A typical engagement begins with a written plan covering users, sites, access and rollback thinking, and then a pilot or first-wave before a wide deployment. It is usually a good fit for companies replacing a fleet of laptops or phones. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. Only if the numbers, the builds and the logistics genuinely allow it. We would rather propose waves than a theatrical 'big bang' that fails at noon. We can use your existing build process or help you define one. The method is part of the plan. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

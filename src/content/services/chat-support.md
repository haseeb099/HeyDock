## Staff the chat channel, and know when to leave it

Chat is a useful way for staff or customers to ask for help, but only if someone trained is actually there, and only if there is a path off the thread when the issue is a break/fix, an account change, or a complaint that needs a record. HeyDock provides staffed chat support as a managed service, for internal IT queries or for technical customer questions, with the hours, language and allowed actions written down.

We do not treat an unattended widget as a service. We also do not hide people behind a pretend assistant. If you want automation, that is a separate, bounded AI conversation, and a human still needs to own the difficult cases.

## Who this is for

This is for teams that already know they will receive questions in chat and want that work supervised. It is not a general call centre for sales, and it is not a replacement for a full helpdesk if most work arrives by email. Many clients use chat as one channel of a helpdesk.

## What we deliver

We staff the agreed hours, follow your guidance, record conversations in an agreed tool, and hand over to tickets or engineers when chat is the wrong place to finish the job. Supervision exists so quality does not depend on one unsupervised shift.

Access to your systems is limited to what the work requires. We will not claim 24/7 presence unless it is staffed.

## How the work runs

Define, prepare, staff, review. Transcripts are used to improve guidance and training, not to surprise people. If volume is too high for the hours you bought, we will say so rather than let queues rot.

Related services include the wider helpdesk, IT support, remote technical talent, and AI automation if you want assistance behind the human, not instead of one.

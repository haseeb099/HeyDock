## Build only what a product cannot reasonably do, and plan who will maintain it

Custom software is justified when a process is distinctive, or when systems must be connected in a way a catalogue product will not allow. HeyDock designs and builds applications, integrations and websites for UK organisations on that basis. We will also tell you when a ready-made product is the better first move.

'Done' includes a handover. An application with no owner, no access list and no support idea is a future incident, not a successful project.

## Who this is for

Teams whose process does not fit a box, organisations that need integrations, and businesses whose website is part of a workflow, not only a brochure. Vendor selection and the software catalogue are the alternatives when buying is wiser than building.

## What we deliver

Discovery, a written first slice, staged build you can see, and a handover or support arrangement. Intellectual property and hosting are written down. We prefer to work with your people rather than disappear into a black box.

## How the work runs

Discover, build, handover, maintain if asked. Stages are short enough that you can change direction. If the first slice shows that a product would have been enough, we will say so even if that reduces the build.

Use this page when you need something made, and you are willing to own it afterwards.

## Practical limits and how to start

Build the software you actually need, in stages, with a plan for maintenance so the result does not become an orphan. A typical engagement begins with a discovery that produces a written scope and a first slice, and then design and build of the agreed application, integration or site. It is usually a good fit for teams whose process does not fit a standard product. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. No. If a ready-made product will do, we will say so and point you to the business software page or a vendor selection piece of work. That is written in the contract before serious build starts. You should not have to guess. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

HeyDock is the customer-facing brand of Infintree Limited. Work is planned in English unless we have agreed otherwise. On-site attendance, where it applies, is confirmed per location rather than assumed from a coverage map.

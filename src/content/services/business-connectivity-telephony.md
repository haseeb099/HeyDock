## Keep the site reachable, and know who actually provides the line

Connectivity and telephony are easy to ignore until the card machines stop or customers hear a dead tone. HeyDock helps UK businesses understand the circuits they already have, plan something more resilient where it is justified, and move to or tidy up cloud telephony. We are not the hidden carrier. We say who provides the line, who provides the numbers, and what we are responsible for.

Many offices run on a single broadband link, a router nobody owns, and a phone system that was installed by someone who has left. The first useful piece of work is often a map, not a new contract.

## Who this is for

This is for offices on a single unmonitored circuit, teams leaving a traditional PBX, and multi-site groups that have inherited a mix of suppliers. If the internal network is the problem, start with network support. If calling quality is actually a meeting-room or Wi-Fi issue, those pages should be in the conversation too.

## What we deliver

We assess how you connect and how you take calls, advise on options including the limits, coordinate with suppliers, and test what we have agreed to test. Resilience is described honestly: a second path helps only if it is designed, powered and occasionally tested.

We will not promise a number port on a date we cannot control. We will not wrap a carrier contract in language that hides who you are actually buying from.

## How the work runs

Map, advise, coordinate, test. Changes that can drop voice or payments belong in a window. Aftercare can sit with monitoring and managed IT so a quiet circuit failure does not wait for a customer complaint.

Microsoft 365 often sits nearby if you are using Teams. Use this page when the essential need is 'can people reach us, and can we reach them?'

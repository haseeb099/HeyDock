## Prepare controls and evidence, and leave certification to the people who can issue it

Compliance support, here, means helping you build and show the controls that ISO 27001, GDPR (as a practical matter) or SOC 2-style customers expect. HeyDock does that work for UK organisations. We do not issue certificates, we do not act as your solicitor, and we will not guarantee an audit outcome.

A policy that no one follows is not evidence. A folder of screenshots with no owner is not a system. We would rather help you adopt a smaller set of living controls than draft a novel nobody will open.

## Who this is for

Teams facing a customer questionnaire, organisations starting a formal framework, and companies that need practical help alongside their legal advisers. This is not a substitute for a Data Protection Officer or an external auditor.

## What we deliver

A gap look, tighter policies that match reality, help with control design and evidence, and dry-run preparation. The scope can be as small as one questionnaire.

We will be explicit about what we cannot sign.

## How the work runs

Choose the target, find the gaps, build, prepare. Work is ordered so you can staff it. Security and consulting pages sit nearby when the issue is broader than a framework.

Use this page when you need to be able to show how you work, honestly, to an auditor or a customer.

## Practical limits and how to start

Turn a framework into a set of controls and evidence you can actually maintain, without offering a certification we cannot issue. A typical engagement begins with a gap look against the framework you care about, and then help writing or tightening policies that match how you work. It is usually a good fit for teams facing a customer security questionnaire they cannot currently answer. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

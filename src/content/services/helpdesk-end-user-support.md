## Give people a proper place to ask for help

A helpdesk is the first line that staff actually use: password resets, new starter laptops, 'Outlook will not open', access to a shared mailbox, a departing employee who still has a login. HeyDock can run or extend that function for UK organisations, with a documented channel, a list of request types, and a path into deeper technical support.

Without a desk, those requests land on whoever is nearest, and they are forgotten when that person is on leave. A modest, well-scoped desk is usually better than a theatre of 24/7 branding with no one behind it.

## Who this is for

This suits growing companies whose staff currently message one busy colleague, and IT teams that want specialists freed from first-line repetition. If you only want live chat as a channel, see chat support. If you want the whole estate monitored and patched, see managed IT.

## What we deliver

We agree the channel, the hours, the request types, and the escalation path. We handle in-scope tickets and keep a simple record of volume and themes. Onboarding and offboarding can be included so starters and leavers follow the same steps. We can often work inside a ticketing tool you already have.

We will not promise a first-reply time we cannot meet. Language, hours and location of the people doing the work are stated in the proposal.

## How the work runs

Channels and a request catalogue come first, then the desk goes live, then we look at repeats. A question that appears twenty times is a candidate for a short guide or a change to the system, not another twenty identical tickets.

This service pairs naturally with IT support, chat support, and business software such as a ticketing system if you need one.

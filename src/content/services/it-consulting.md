## Get a plan you can take to a budget holder, including the option to stop

IT consulting, as we practise it, is the work of turning a messy situation into options and a sequence. HeyDock does that for UK organisations. The output should be usable even if you never hire us to deliver the work. 'Buy this from us' is not a consulting conclusion on its own.

We listen to the people doing the work, write the constraints down, and include our doubts as well as our recommendation.

## Who this is for

Leaders with a problem larger than a ticket, teams caught between vendors, and organisations that want a plan before a hire or a purchase. Specialist pages cover transformation, architecture, vendor selection, cost, continuity, post-merger work and site surveys. Start here when the problem is still a shape rather than a named project.

## What we deliver

Conversations, a current-state view, options, a recommended sequence, and a short decision pack. Delivery is optional and separately briefed.

We will not bury a sales pitch in a 80-page appendix.

## How the work runs

Listen, frame, recommend, decide. If a site survey or a technical deep dive is required, we will say so rather than guess.

Use this page when you need a plan more than you need a product.

## Practical limits and how to start

Turn a messy technology problem into a plan with options, a sequence and an honest view of cost and effort. A typical engagement begins with workshops or interviews with the people who know the problem, and then a written view of the current state and the options. It is usually a good fit for leaders with a problem that is bigger than a single ticket. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. No. A good consulting outcome can be 'do nothing', 'buy a product', or 'hire someone'. We will say that when it is true. You receive a written plan you can use even if you never hire us again. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

HeyDock is the customer-facing brand of Infintree Limited. Work is planned in English unless we have agreed otherwise. On-site attendance, where it applies, is confirmed per location rather than assumed from a coverage map.

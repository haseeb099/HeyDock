## Make the IT spend explainable, then cut only what you can defend

IT cost work is useful when the bill can be explained, and when a proposed cut comes with the operational risk attached. HeyDock helps UK organisations tidy avoidable spend and tell a clearer budget story, across licences, contracts, infrastructure and, where relevant, people. We will not promise a percentage before we have seen the numbers.

Unused licences, overlapping tools, and 'temporary' platforms that became permanent are common. So is underinvestment that looks cheap until something fails.

## Who this is for

Finance and IT leads building a budget, organisations with tool sprawl, and teams whose spend has not been revisited after growth. Cloud-only cost work can start on the FinOps page.

## What we deliver

A readable cost view, a list of options with risks, a budget narrative, and a follow-up so savings are observed. Holes in the data are stated.

We will not recommend cutting support without saying what that would feel like.

## How the work runs

Collect, explain, propose, plan. You remain the commercial decision maker. Vendor selection and managed services often sit next door when a change of model is the real saving.

Use this page when the essential need is an honest, organisation-wide view of IT cost.

## Practical limits and how to start

Find avoidable IT spend and plan a budget you can explain, without cutting things you will need on a busy Tuesday. A typical engagement begins with a readable view of major IT costs (licences, contracts, infrastructure, people as relevant), and then identification of waste, overlap and unused commitments. It is usually a good fit for finance and IT leads preparing a budget with incomplete data. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

This service can include cloud, but it also covers licences, contracts, on-premises and other IT spend. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

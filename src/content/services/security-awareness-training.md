## Teach people how to notice and report, not how to feel blamed

Security awareness training is useful when it is short, specific, and tied to the way your organisation actually communicates and pays people. HeyDock designs and delivers that training for UK staff. A once-a-year generic video is better than nothing, and it is usually not enough.

We focus on recognition and reporting. We do not run humiliation exercises, and we do not treat a failed quiz as a disciplinary event.

## Who this is for

Organisations with only a token induction video, teams that have had a scare, and IT leads who want more reports and fewer silent mistakes. Simulations and AI-driven phishing defence are related and optional; they have stricter consent and framing rules.

## What we deliver

Context-specific sessions or materials, a clear reporting route, optional checks, and a simple record of delivery. Content covers the channels you use: email, chat, phone, payment processes.

We will not claim that training 'eliminates human risk'. People remain people.

## How the work runs

Context, design, deliver, follow up. If you also want simulations, they are briefed separately and ethically. Helpdesk staff often need a slightly different session because they are a target and a reporting point.

Use this page for teaching. Use the simulation and phishing-defence pages for authorised exercises and technical controls.

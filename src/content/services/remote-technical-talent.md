## Add remote specialists who already know how they will work with you

Remote technical talent is useful when the work can be done away from the site, and when the hours, systems and escalation path are agreed in advance. HeyDock introduces IT, software and support specialists into UK teams on that basis. The person is not a vague 'extra pair of hands'. They are briefed on the stack, the overlap with your people, and who they call when something is beyond the agreed scope.

This is the service many MSPs and internal IT teams actually want: someone who can join the ticket queue, the build, or the cloud work for agreed hours, without pretending they can also drive to a branch office the same afternoon.

## Who this is for

UK businesses and partners that can name the systems and the hours do best here. If you need a hire in another country with a formal employment model, see remote and global recruitment. If you need a visitor on a site, see engineer dispatch. If you need an always-on helpdesk, see helpdesk or managed IT, which are service contracts rather than an embedded specialist.

## What we deliver

We agree scope, find a suitable specialist or shortlist, and plan access so the first day is not spent waiting for an account. Working under your brand and tools is normal when you want that. We set a named escalation path so the person is not left as the last resort for problems they cannot solve.

We will not claim 24/7 coverage unless it is staffed and written down. We will not treat a start date as booked until commercial terms are clear.

## How the work runs

Scope first, then match, then access, then a short period of closer check-ins. If the work changes, we revisit the brief rather than silently expanding it. If the arrangement is not working, it is better to say so early.

This service sits beside IT support, custom software and engineer dispatch. Use it when the main need is skilled remote capacity inside your way of working.

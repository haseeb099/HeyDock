## Watch the signals you have agreed, and be precise about the night hours

Security operations means someone is looking at the right alerts, during the hours you have actually bought, and knows what to do next. HeyDock can provide that monitoring and triage for agreed identity, email, endpoint or network sources. We will not sell a '24/7 SOC' label if the night hours are only a tool running unattended.

Logs that nobody reads are not a detection programme. Neither is a queue of 4,000 low-value alerts.

## Who this is for

Organisations with logs but no reader, and teams that want a first line of triage. Infrastructure monitoring remains a separate, uptime-focused service. Incident response is what happens when an event is serious. AI-driven phishing defence can feed this work; it does not replace it.

## What we deliver

Agreed sources, tuned alerts, triage in contracted hours, an incident path, and reviews that include hardening. Tool choice is proportionate. Out-of-hours behaviour is explicit.

We will not pretend we can see attacks we are not collecting signals for.

## How the work runs

Sources, detect, triage, improve. When something serious is found, we follow the written path rather than improvising a press statement. If the estate changes, the detections should change too.

Use this page when you need a watched, honest security monitoring function.

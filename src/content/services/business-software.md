## Buy a product when a product will do, and implement it so people actually use it

Not every problem needs a custom application. HR records, stock and invoices, a service desk, bookings and appointments are often better served by a proven product, implemented carefully. HeyDock helps UK organisations choose and put those systems to work: HR management, small-business ERP, ticketing and service desk, booking management, and appointment scheduling.

The failure mode is familiar: a licence is bought, a spreadsheet remains the source of truth, and nobody owns the new system. Implementation, training and a named owner are part of the work.

## Who this is for

Teams buying a first serious operations system, companies outgrowing a spreadsheet, and leads who want a product rather than a long build. If the process is too distinctive, custom software is the better page. If you need help comparing vendors more widely, see vendor selection.

## What we deliver

A match to a category, a selection if more than one product could fit, implementation, proportionate migration, and training. We will not stretch a catalogue item past its honest limit.

## How the work runs

Match, select, implement, adopt. The first weeks after go-live matter as much as the configuration. Support can continue under a written arrangement.

Use this page when a ready-made system is the right shape for the work.

## Practical limits and how to start

Select and implement ready-made software for HR, operations, service desks and scheduling when a catalogue product is a better fit than a custom build. A typical engagement begins with help matching a problem to a product category, and then implementation and configuration of agreed systems. It is usually a good fit for small and mid-sized teams buying their first serious operations system. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. We will not force a product because it is on a list. If another system is a better fit, we will say so. HR management, small-business ERP, ticketing and service desk, booking management, and appointment scheduling are the current catalogue. Other categories can be discussed. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

HeyDock is the customer-facing brand of Infintree Limited. Work is planned in English unless we have agreed otherwise. On-site attendance, where it applies, is confirmed per location rather than assumed from a coverage map.

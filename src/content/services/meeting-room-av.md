## Make the meeting room boring, in the best sense

A meeting room should start a call without a ceremony. When it does not, people bring their laptops, flood the table with dongles, and blame the platform. HeyDock supports workplace meeting rooms in the UK: displays, cameras, speakers, conferencing devices and the simple standard that makes one room behave like the next.

We look at how people actually start a meeting, not only whether a device pings. Quite often the fix is a cable, a firmware update, a wireless improvement, or a single agreed way to join, rather than a new wall of equipment.

## Who this is for

Offices with rooms people avoid, IT and facilities leads who have inherited mixed kit, and organisations standardising on a conferencing platform. This is not a festival or theatre AV service. If the network or wireless is failing the room, those services should be included.

## What we deliver

We agree what 'working' means, inspect, fix what we can, and propose a standard or replacement where the kit is the limit. A short user note is part of the job. Upgrades are only done when you approve them.

We will not claim every manufacturer certification if we do not have it. We will not hide a network problem inside an AV quote.

## How the work runs

List, inspect, fix or standardise, hand over. A single room is a valid start. If you have many rooms, we suggest a pattern rather than twenty unique specials.

Microsoft 365 and Teams often sit nearby. Engineer dispatch is how we attend. Use this page when the essential need is a room that works for the people who book it.

## Plan wireless for the rooms people actually sit in

Business Wi-Fi fails in predictable ways: a domestic router in a comms cupboard, access points clustered in the wrong hallway, a guest network that is actually the staff network, a meeting room that looks fine on a drawing and is unusable on a video call. HeyDock surveys, installs and improves workplace wireless in the UK so coverage follows how the space is used.

A survey is not optional if the problem is coverage or density. Buying more access points without walking the floor is how you spend money and keep the same complaints.

## Who this is for

Offices with dead spots, hospitality and retail sites, and teams moving floor or building. If the cables, switches or firewall are the weak point, pair this with network support. If the incoming service is slow or unstable, see business connectivity. Meeting room troubles are often a mix of wireless, display and the room itself; see meeting room and AV as well.

## What we deliver

We survey, recommend placement and cabling, install or coordinate the install, separate guest and staff access in a business-appropriate way, and verify the rooms you care about. We will be honest if the building (thick walls, listed constraints, a landlord cupboard) limits what radio can do.

We do not promise 'seamless roaming everywhere' as a slogan. We promise a design that matches the brief, and a check against that brief at the end.

## How the work runs

Walk, survey, install, verify. Landlord or cabling vendors may be involved; we will not pretend we can drill a listed wall without permission. Aftercare can sit inside network support or managed IT if you want an ongoing owner.

Use this page when the problem is wireless experience, not only 'the internet is down'.

## Sequence the work, name the owners, and allow yourself to stop

Digital transformation is a loaded phrase. Here it means a realistic sequence of technology and process change, with owners and checkpoints, in service of goals you can still recognise next year. HeyDock helps UK organisations write that kind of roadmap. We will also tell you what to stop.

A pile of initiatives without an order is not a strategy. Neither is a glossy programme that nobody has time to run.

## Who this is for

Leaders with too many starts and not enough finishes, and teams that need a multi-year sequence rather than a single project plan. If you need a one-problem plan, IT consulting may be enough. Architecture, post-merger work and AI pilots often become items on the roadmap rather than the whole of it.

## What we deliver

Goals, a map of current work, a sequenced roadmap, and a light review habit. The plan should survive contact with a budget cycle.

We will not staff a theatrical 'transformation office' you did not ask for.

## How the work runs

Goals, map, sequence, govern. If delivery follows, it is briefed in slices. The roadmap is a living document, not a poster.

Use this page when the essential need is an honest sequence of change.

## Practical limits and how to start

Sequence technology change against business goals, with owners and checkpoints, rather than a single grand programme with no off-ramp. A typical engagement begins with a view of the goals that actually matter to the organisation, and then a map of current systems and work already in flight. It is usually a good fit for leaders who have too many initiatives and not enough finish. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. Not necessarily. We will not invent a bureaucracy you cannot staff. A simple review meeting is often enough. Delivery can follow, under a separate brief. The roadmap should still make sense if someone else delivers it. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

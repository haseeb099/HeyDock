## Let AI help with volume, and keep a human responsible for the decision

Phishing remains a common way in, and the volume can exhaust a small team. HeyDock helps UK organisations use AI-assisted detection, triage and coaching to deal with that volume, alongside a reporting path and a playbook that still work when the model is wrong. A person remains responsible for serious decisions. The tool is an assistant.

We will not claim that AI 'solves phishing'. We will not silently read a whole mail estate if a narrower, platform-native control will do.

## Who this is for

Teams overwhelmed by 'is this safe?' reports, and organisations turning on AI features in a mail platform without a governance idea. Simulations remain a separate, ethical service. Microsoft 365 is often the platform under discussion. Security operations can take the serious alerts.

## What we deliver

A map of the current path, help choosing or tuning tools, playbooks, optional coaching that does not shame people, and a review of misses and false positives. Staff should be told how reporting works and what automation might do to their mail.

## How the work runs

Map, select, playbooks, review. Go-live is not the end. If the tool blocks too much, people will route around it, and we will treat that as a design failure.

Use this page for defensive technology and process. Use awareness and authorised simulation for the human practice that sits beside it.

## Start calmly, contain with authority, and write down what you know

Incident response is the structured work of a suspected security event: a mailbox that sent strange mail, a ransomware note, a supplier telling you their system that holds your data was involved, an admin account that looks wrong. HeyDock helps UK organisations triage, contain, investigate and recover, and produce a timeline they can later stand behind. We will not shout, and we will not promise a full forensic reconstruction if the logs were never kept.

The first hour is often about slowing down: who can decide, what must not be wiped, and what can be safely disconnected.

## Who this is for

Teams who think something is wrong, and leaders who will have to explain the next few days. If you have no incident and want monitoring, see security operations. If you need to restore business operations after a wider outage, business continuity and disaster recovery sit alongside this work.

## What we deliver

A first-response conversation, containment within your authority, evidence guidance, investigation to the depth access allows, and a written timeline with next steps. Whether to call insurers, legal advisers or authorities is your decision; we can help you have the facts ready.

We will not advise you to pay a ransom. We will not invent certainty the evidence does not support.

## How the work runs

Triage, contain, investigate, recover and record. If we are on a retainer, contacts and access should already exist. If we are called cold, we start by establishing authority and a communications path.

Use this page when you have a suspected incident, not when you only want a general health check.

We can help you assemble a timeline; legal and regulatory decisions remain yours, with your advisers. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

## Make the network visible, then change it on purpose

Network support is the work of keeping the office, the links and the firewalls understandable and reliable. HeyDock helps UK businesses when a fault has appeared, when the network has grown without a drawing, or when a change is needed and nobody wants to be the person who 'just tweaked a rule'. We start by seeing the network as it really is, not as last year's diagram says it is.

A surprising number of incidents come from an undocumented change or a switch that was never added to monitoring. We would rather spend the first engagement documenting and stabilising than proposing a replacement stack you do not need.

## Who this is for

This is for offices whose network has grown organically, IT teams facing a difficult fault, and businesses opening a site or replacing ageing switches. If the main symptom is bad wireless, start with the Wi-Fi page as well. If the issue is the incoming circuit, see business connectivity. If you need overnight alerting, see infrastructure monitoring.

We are not a product reseller first. If you need new hardware we can help you specify it and, if you wish, work with your supplier. You should not have to buy a brand to get a diagnosis.

## What we deliver

We review or create practical documentation, troubleshoot faults, and recommend changes with a window and a back-out idea. Firewall advice stays at a level that matches the risk: we will not pretend a single rule change is a zero-trust programme, and we will not leave a wide-open rule 'temporarily' without saying so.

Credentials are handled carefully. We do not take standing admin access we do not need. Vendor liaison is included where you already have a circuit or hardware supplier and want us to be the technical voice.

## How the work runs

See, stabilise, recommend, change. Live faults come before tidy-up. Change windows are agreed with you, especially if the work can drop voice or payments. After a significant change we confirm that users can work, not only that a dashboard is green.

Network support is close to Wi-Fi, connectivity, monitoring and, when you are ready, a more deliberate access model such as zero trust. Use this page when the wired network and the perimeter need a steady, documented hand.

## Practical limits and how to start

Design, operate and troubleshoot LAN, WAN, switching, firewalls and office connectivity, with a written picture of the network before we change it. A typical engagement begins with review or documentation of the current network, and then troubleshooting of faults and slow or unstable links. It is usually a good fit for offices whose network has grown by adding boxes without a plan. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. Only if that is agreed and we have a rollback plan. We do not seize control of a live perimeter as a first step. We will tell you which platforms we can support for your brief. If a platform needs a specialist or the vendor, we will say so. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later.

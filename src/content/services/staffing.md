## Pick a staffing model that matches the work, then hire against it

Not every need is a permanent job. Some work lasts twelve weeks. Some roles should be on the payroll. Some sit in the middle: a project with a defined outcome and a small team. HeyDock helps UK businesses and MSPs choose a contract, permanent or project staffing model and then fill it from a written brief.

The model should follow the work. A two-month migration does not automatically need a permanent hire. A core support role that will still exist in three years often should not be an endless chain of short contracts. We will talk through those trade-offs plainly, including management effort and budget, without pretending there is a single right answer.

## Who this is for

This page is for managers who already know they need people but are not yet sure how to engage them. It is also for programme leads who need extra hands for a defined period, and for companies that want a reusable way to brief HeyDock when similar needs come up again.

If you already know you want a permanent technical hire, the IT staffing page may be enough. If the people will work only from overseas, start with remote and global recruitment. This page is about the engagement model itself.

## What we deliver

We help you compare models, write the brief, search, and place people under written terms. During a contract or project we remain a named contact if the scope shifts. Near the end we discuss extension, conversion or a clean finish so nobody is left guessing.

We do not give tax or employment-law advice. IR35, holiday pay and employer obligations need proper advice for your situation. We will not start someone on a handshake.

## How the work runs

First we choose the model. Then we hire in the usual way: brief, search, shortlist, decision. You remain the decision maker. An enquiry does not lock you into a fee or a start date. If the first model was wrong, we would rather change it than force a bad fit.

## Practical limits and how to start

Choose a contract, permanent or project staffing model once the work, duration and budget are clear, then hire against a written brief. A typical engagement begins with advice on contract versus permanent versus project models, and then written role and commercial outline before a start date is treated as booked. It is usually a good fit for managers who know the work but are unsure whether to hire or contract. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. Sometimes, if both sides agree and the original terms allow it. Conversion should be discussed early, not improvised at the end of a contract. Arrangements vary. Some people contract through their own company, some through an agency model. This is agreed in writing; we do not give tax advice. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

## Run Microsoft 365 as a system, not a pile of default settings

Microsoft 365 becomes the backbone of email, files and identity for many UK organisations, often without anyone quite taking ownership of the tenant. HeyDock helps you deploy, secure and support that environment: the identity model, the sharing defaults, the admin roles, and the migrations that move people without a weekend of guesswork.

Default settings are not a security strategy. Neither is turning on every control on a Monday morning and locking out the finance team. We sequence the work and explain what people will feel.

## Who this is for

Teams with an unmanaged tenant, organisations moving from another platform, and internal IT that wants a partner for changes and incidents. Meeting room hardware is a sibling service. Wider Azure architecture may sit under cloud solutions.

## What we deliver

Assessment or a new deployment plan, configuration of identity and collaboration, agreed security baselines, migrations where briefed, and a support path afterwards. Licence purchasing can be discussed but is not the centre of the work.

We will not claim a Microsoft specialisation we cannot stand behind. We will not hide a messy tenant behind a new logo.

## How the work runs

Assess, plan, implement, support. Changes that affect sign-in or sharing go in a window, with a way to reach us if something breaks. After a project, ongoing support can join IT support or cloud management.

Use this page when Microsoft 365 is the product you need to deploy, tidy or live with confidently.

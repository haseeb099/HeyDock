## Choose a cloud direction from the workloads, not from a trend

Cloud solutions work when they start from what you already run, what it costs, and who will operate it on a Tuesday morning. HeyDock helps UK organisations make those decisions across AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, then design and deliver the work that follows. We will not recommend a platform because it is fashionable, and we will not treat 'move everything' as a strategy.

A useful first engagement is often a map: applications, data, identity, networks and the people who currently keep them alive. Only then does a target picture mean anything.

## Who this is for

This is for teams that arrived in a cloud almost by accident, for organisations choosing a platform for a new workload, and for leads who want a challenge to a plan they have already been sold. If you already know you need a migration, a management contract, a pipeline, or a cost review, those pages go deeper.

## What we deliver

We review workloads, recommend a direction with reasons, produce an architecture outline you can challenge, and deliver or coordinate agreed work. Project work is split from later operations so a build does not quietly become an unpaid support contract.

We do not invent certifications we cannot show, and we do not hide vendor lock-in where it exists. You should be able to read the recommendation in ordinary language.

## How the work runs

Workloads, options, design, deliver. You remain the decision maker on spend and risk. After delivery, cloud management, FinOps or DevOps can take over if you want them.

Use this page for the overall direction. Use the more specific cloud pages when the need is already clear.

## Practical limits and how to start

Design and deliver cloud work on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud that matches your workloads and budget, rather than a platform fashion. A typical engagement begins with a look at current workloads and where they live today, and then a recommended direction on AWS, Azure, Google Cloud or a mix, with reasons. It is usually a good fit for organisations that have landed in a cloud without a plan. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. We work with AWS, Azure and Google Cloud as the brief requires. We will not force a platform change for its own sake. We may help you buy through existing programmes, but the advice should stand if you purchase another way. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

HeyDock is the customer-facing brand of Infintree Limited. Work is planned in English unless we have agreed otherwise. On-site attendance, where it applies, is confirmed per location rather than assumed from a coverage map.

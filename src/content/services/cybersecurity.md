## Reduce obvious risk, and agree what you will do when something fails

Cybersecurity, as HeyDock offers it, is a practical programme: look at the controls you have, fix the gaps that matter first, and write down how you will respond when something still goes wrong. We work with UK businesses and MSPs who want that kind of help, not a claim of invulnerability.

Phishing, weak identity, unpatched systems, missing backups and no one to call at a bad moment are still the usual problems. A tool catalogue does not replace those basics.

## Who this is for

Organisations that have never had a structured look, IT leads with a long list of vendor advice, and directors who want a plain picture. Deeper services (testing, SOC, incident response, awareness, phishing defence, zero trust, compliance) sit on their own pages and can be added once priorities are clear.

## What we deliver

An assessment of what is actually in place, a short prioritised list, help implementing agreed changes, and a simple incident path. We will not promise that you will not be breached. We will not invent certifications.

## How the work runs

Look, prioritise, improve, maintain. Quick wins are not a substitute for a later review. If testing or monitoring is justified, we will say so rather than expand quietly.

Use this page as the front door to the security practice. Use the specialist pages when you already know the kind of work you need.

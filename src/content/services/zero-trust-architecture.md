## Verify access on purpose, and phase the change so people can still work

Zero trust, in plain language, means we do not treat a user or a device as safe just because they appeared on the office network or used a password once last year. HeyDock helps UK organisations design and phase that kind of access model, usually starting with identity and device health, then network segmentation where it is justified.

The phrase is used loosely in the market. We will define it for your context in a page of ordinary language before anyone buys a tool.

## Who this is for

Organisations that still trust the office VLAN by default, teams with an identity platform that has never been tightened, and leads who have been told to 'do zero trust' without a definition. Microsoft 365 work often sits underneath. Network support is the sibling when segmentation is in play.

## What we deliver

A definition, a current-state look, a phased target, and implementation of agreed phases with a back-out idea. User impact is described in advance. The model is documented so it can be operated when we are not in the room.

We will not lock an entire company out in a single weekend to make a point.

## How the work runs

Define, assess, design, implement. Each phase has a support path. Compliance conversations can reuse the same design if you later need to show it.

Use this page when the essential need is a better access model, not a new slogan.

## Practical limits and how to start

Design access around verification, least privilege and segmentation, starting from your current identity and network, not a slogan. A typical engagement begins with a current-state look at identity, devices and network trust, and then a target access model you can phase in. It is usually a good fit for organisations still treating the office network as trusted by default. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

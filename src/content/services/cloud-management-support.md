## Keep a cloud estate operable after the project photos are over

Cloud management is the work that starts when the migration slides are filed: who approves a change, who is watching the bill, who still has admin, and who answers when a workload dies at 7am. HeyDock provides ongoing management and support for agreed AWS, Azure or Google Cloud estates in the UK. The service exists because project teams leave, and because a cloud account is not self-herding.

We begin with a handover that may have to be reconstructed. If nobody can draw the estate, that is the first deliverable, not a footnote.

## Who this is for

Teams that have landed in a cloud and feel unattended, companies whose build partner has moved on, and internal IT that wants overflow. If you are still choosing a platform, start with cloud solutions. If you need a migration, use that page. If cost is the only pain, FinOps may be the better front door.

## What we deliver

Support for agreed accounts, a change process, scoped hygiene such as backups and access reviews, incident help at the cloud layer, and reviews that include cost and postponed work. Vendor support plans remain theirs; we help you use them.

Access is least-privilege and recorded. New product work is called out as a project.

## How the work runs

Handover, baseline, operate, review. Hours and response are written down. Monitoring can sit alongside if you want alerting as well as a ticket path.

Use this page when you need an operator, not another strategy workshop.

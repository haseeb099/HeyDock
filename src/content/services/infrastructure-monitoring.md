## Know about a failure before the office does, and be honest about the night shift

Infrastructure monitoring is the unexciting work of checking that servers, links, and critical services are still up, and of telling a named person when they are not. HeyDock can set up and run that monitoring for agreed systems. The important distinction is between a check that runs at night and a human who will act at night. We will not bundle those together in the small print.

If you only want a dashboard, that is a smaller engagement. If you want someone to respond, that response has to be scoped, staffed and written down.

## Who this is for

This is for teams that currently learn about outages from their users, and for small IT functions that cannot watch graphs all day. It also suits MSPs that want a monitoring layer for a client without pretending they have a global operations centre they do not have.

If your main concern is intrusion rather than uptime, see security operations. If you need a full managed service including patching and tickets, see managed IT.

## What we deliver

We inventory the estate, put checks in place, route alerts to people who can actually receive them, and tune the noise. Informational alerts and critical alerts are separated. Out-of-hours behaviour is explicit: monitor only, or monitor and respond.

We test that an alert arrives, rather than assuming the tool is enough. We do not auto-change production systems without a written runbook.

## How the work runs

Inventory, instrument, tune, operate. When you add a server or a site, the checks should change too. A quarterly look at noisy alerts is more valuable than a wall of ignored email.

This service supports managed IT, network support, and business continuity planning. Use it when the first need is visibility and honest alerting, not a slogan about being always on.

## Keep people working when devices and accounts fail

IT support is the work of getting a person or a small office back to a usable state: a laptop that will not start, an account that is locked, a shared drive that has vanished, a printer that no longer appears, a Wi-Fi problem that only happens in one room. HeyDock provides on-site and remote IT support for UK businesses and for MSPs that need extra capacity, including white-label delivery where that is agreed.

We start with the systems and hours you want covered. Support that tries to cover 'everything, always' without a list is how tickets disappear and tempers rise. A shorter, honest scope is more useful than a wide promise.

## Who this is for

This is for organisations that want a practical way to raise faults and have them worked, without immediately buying a full managed service. It is also for partners who need an engineer or a remote technician for their own clients. If you only need a planned rollout, use the rollout page. If you only need a broken device swapped, hardware break/fix may be enough.

## What we deliver

Remote troubleshooting comes first when it can solve the issue. If it cannot, we arrange a visit, with access and a site contact agreed in advance. You get a named way to raise work. When something is beyond the agreed skill or needs a vendor, we escalate with a written picture of what has already been tried.

We do not advertise a response time we have not staffed. We do not treat a visit as booked until location, skills and access are confirmed.

## How the work runs

Scope, connect, support, review. Repeat incidents are a signal: sometimes the answer is a fix, sometimes training, sometimes a move to managed IT. You decide whether to widen the arrangement.

Related pages cover dispatch-only visits, a fuller helpdesk, monitoring, and hardware repair. Use this page when the everyday need is 'please make it work again', both on-site and remote.

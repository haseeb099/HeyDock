## Hire remote people with the constraints written down first

Remote and global recruitment fails when the only brief is to find someone cheaper overseas. HeyDock helps UK organisations hire remote or overseas staff when the work, the hours of overlap, and the employment model have been written down. That is how you avoid a shortlist of people who cannot attend the stand-up, cannot be paid cleanly, or cannot reach the systems they need.

We treat location as a constraint, not a marketing claim. If a role needs four hours of overlap with a London team, that belongs in the brief. If a country is impractical for tax or contracting reasons, we will say so rather than pretend the world is a single labour pool.

## Who this is for

This is for UK businesses and MSPs that want to add remote capacity (developers, support analysts, cloud engineers or, where agreed, non-technical roles) and are willing to be specific about how the person will work. It is also for teams that have already tried informal overseas hiring and want a clearer process.

It is not a visa advisory service, and it is not a promise that every role can be done from anywhere. Some work has to happen on a site in the UK. In those cases we will point you to on-site support or local staffing instead.

## What we deliver

We agree the geography, overlap hours and employment model, then run a search with screening that includes remote-working practice as well as technical skill. The shortlist explains those points so you are not discovering time-zone issues in the final interview.

We can discuss contractor versus employment options in practical terms, but we do not provide legal, tax or immigration advice. If you need an employer-of-record or a local entity, that has to be arranged properly, not improvised in a chat.

## How the work runs

The first conversation is about constraints. Only then do we write the role brief and start sourcing. Interviews can be remote. If you hire, onboarding needs more care than a local start: access, equipment, data handling and a named UK contact. We help you plan those steps so the person is not left waiting for a laptop or a login.

Related services cover purely technical remote talent, general staffing models, and mixed technical and non-technical hiring. Use this page when location and remote working are the defining features of the search.

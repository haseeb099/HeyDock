## Book the site visit after the skills and access are confirmed

Engineer dispatch is how you get a competent person to a door: a branch office, a retail unit, a small warehouse, a professional practice. HeyDock arranges planned on-site IT work for UK businesses and for MSPs who need white-label attendance. The important rule is simple. A visit is not booked until the city, the skills, the window and the access are confirmed.

We do not publish a coverage heat map and then apologise on the day. European or unusual sites are treated the same way: confirmed per request, including language and scheduling constraints.

## Who this is for

MSPs use this when their own engineers cannot reach a site this week. Internal IT teams use it for offices that have no local staff. Project leads use it for rollouts and moves. If you need an ongoing desk rather than a visit, see IT support or managed IT. If the 'site' is a data centre cage, see data centre support.

## What we deliver

We confirm the request, send an engineer for the agreed task, and report what was done. Work can be white-labelled. Remote support can sit behind the visit so the person on site is not improvising. Parts, downtime and customer-facing behaviour are agreed in the brief, not invented on the pavement.

## How the work runs

Request, confirm, attend, report. If the job grows, the engineer calls, rather than quietly adding hours. Same-day requests are attempted only when they are realistic.

Related work includes break/fix, rollouts, general IT support and data centre smart hands. Use dispatch when the essential thing is a person at a named place, at a named time, doing a named task.

## Recruit the business roles that sit beside the technical work

Not every important hire is an engineer. UK organisations also need coordinators, administrators, operations staff, finance and HR support, customer support leads and other non-technical people who keep the work moving. HeyDock recruits those roles with the same briefing discipline we use for technical searches: write down the work, screen against it, and send a shortlist you can use.

This matters when IT and business hiring are happening at the same time. A project coordinator should not be assessed like a developer. A finance assistant should not receive a technical test. We keep the briefs separate and still give you one place to talk about progress.

## Who this is for

This service is for companies that want help filling business and operations roles, including those that already use HeyDock for technical hiring. It also suits IT leaders who need a business analyst, coordinator or customer-facing support person who is not a hands-on engineer.

We are not a general executive search firm, and we will not claim a nationwide secretarial pool. If a role is senior, highly regulated or outside what we can reasonably reach, we will tell you early.

## What we deliver

We produce a written brief, source candidates, screen for the experience that actually matters, and present a shortlist with notes. Working pattern, customer contact, tools (for example, a finance or HR system) and hours are part of that brief. Contract, permanent and part-time options can be used where they fit.

As with all HeyDock hiring, there is no guaranteed fill time, and an enquiry is not a placement. Terms are confirmed in writing before anyone starts.

## How the work runs

The first conversation is about the work, not the job title. Some 'non-technical' roles still need confidence with business systems or reporting. We will include that in screening without turning the process into an engineering interview. If you are also hiring technical staff, we can run both workstreams and keep them clearly labelled.

Related pages cover technical hiring, general staffing models, and chat support if what you actually need is a managed support function rather than an employee.

## Practical limits and how to start

Recruit business, operations, admin, finance, HR and other non-technical roles alongside the IT practice, from a written brief. A typical engagement begins with role brief for business and operations vacancies, and then sourcing and structured screening. It is usually a good fit for companies that want one staffing partner for mixed teams. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. Many teams are mixed. Coordinators, operations, finance and HR roles often sit alongside IT work. It is cleaner to brief one partner than to split a small campaign. We can discuss leadership roles, but our day-to-day strength is operational and specialist hiring. If a search is outside our reach, we will say so. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

## Practise spotting social engineering, only with authority, and only to teach

Social engineering is the craft of persuading a person to give access, money or information they should not give. Criminals use it. HeyDock does not. This service exists so your own organisation can practise recognising those approaches in an authorised, ethical way, and so people get coaching rather than blame.

We will run simulations only when a responsible owner has signed a purpose, a scope and a list of things we will not do. We will not attack your customers, your suppliers, or the public. We will not use cruel pretexts. We will not produce a humiliation league table.

## Who this is for

Organisations that already take awareness seriously and want a careful exercise, and security leads who have been asked for a 'phishing test' and want to do it without harming trust. If you only want classroom-style teaching, the awareness training page is enough. Technical phishing defence sits on its own page.

## What we deliver

A signed brief, proportionate scenarios on agreed channels, a carefully run exercise, constructive coaching, and a theme-based report. Individual surprise can be part of a test; organisational secrecy from HR or leadership is not.

If we cannot agree ethical rules, we will decline the work.

## How the work runs

Authorise, design, run, coach and report. Staff should have a reporting route before the exercise starts, so the 'right' behaviour is possible. Afterward, training can be updated from the themes we actually saw.

Use this page only for defensive, authorised awareness. That boundary is the service.

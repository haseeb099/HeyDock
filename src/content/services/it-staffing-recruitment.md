## Hire the skills you need, without a vague search

IT staffing works when the role is specific. HeyDock helps UK businesses and MSPs hire engineers, administrators, analysts and other technical people for contract, permanent or project work. We start with a written brief: the systems, the seniority, the working pattern. We search against that, rather than sending a pile of loosely related CVs.

A good search is slower to start and faster to shortlist. We would rather spend time agreeing what the person will actually do in the first three months than waste interview hours on people who cannot do the work. That is true whether you are replacing a leaver, adding capacity for a programme, or standing up a new support line.

## Who this is for

This service suits organisations that know they need technical people but do not want an unfocused market trawl. It is also useful for MSPs that need to add delivery capacity for a client without building a permanent bench they cannot use. If you already have a recruiter, we can still run a specialist search for a hard-to-fill skill.

We work with hiring managers who can describe the work, even if the job title is still being decided. If the role is entirely undefined, we will say so and suggest a short scoping conversation first, which may sit under IT consulting rather than a live vacancy.

## What we deliver

We help you shape the vacancy, source candidates, carry out a structured screen, and present a shortlist with notes. Screening covers the technical claims that matter for the role, not a generic competency grid. Right-to-work and other basic checks are agreed in advance so there are no surprises later.

We do not promise a placement in a set number of days, and we do not invent a bench we cannot evidence. Availability changes. What you can expect is a clear process, honest feedback when the market is thin, and a named person who will tell you if the brief should change.

Contract, permanent and project models are all available. The commercial terms are confirmed in writing before anyone starts. Submitting an enquiry does not create a placement or a fee.

## How a typical search runs

After the brief we agree channels, timescales and what will appear on the shortlist. You stay in control of interviews and the final decision. If the first shortlist is not right, we refine the brief rather than sending more of the same. When you want to hire, we help with start dates, contracting and a simple handover so the person is not left guessing on day one.

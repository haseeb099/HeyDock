## Hire technical people by the work, not just the job title

Technical teams are rarely one shape. You may need a support analyst, a network engineer, a cloud administrator, a security specialist, a developer, a data analyst or an operations lead, sometimes in the same quarter. HeyDock recruits technical staff of all those kinds for UK organisations, using a brief built around the systems and outcomes, not a fashionable title.

This page is the wide door into technical hiring. If you already know you want a remote-only search, or a managed support contract rather than a hire, the related pages will be a better fit. If you need people who can do the technical work, start here.

## Who this is for

IT managers who are hiring into more than one discipline, or who are rebuilding a team after someone leaves, get the most from this service. MSPs use it when a client needs a specialist skill that is not on the current bench. You do not need a perfect job description to start, but you do need to be able to talk about the systems and the problems.

We will not pretend every rare skill is on tap. Some combinations are scarce in the UK market. In those cases we discuss contract versus permanent, remote versus on-site, and whether the work should be a service engagement instead of a hire.

## What we deliver

We map the work to roles, agree seniority and working pattern, and run a search with screening that is specific to the discipline. A network brief is not screened like a development brief. The shortlist explains that difference so interviewers are prepared.

You stay in control of interviews and offers. We help with practical onboarding questions: access, equipment, first-week expectations. Commercial terms are written down before a start date is treated as booked.

## How the work runs

If several roles are open, we help you prioritise so the team can operate while later hires are still in progress. Each search has a named contact. When the market is thin we will say so and suggest a change to the brief, rather than quietly lowering the bar.

For non-technical roles, use the companion page. For a single remote specialist, remote technical talent may be more precise. For an ongoing support contract instead of a hire, see managed IT and IT support.

## Practical limits and how to start

Hire across the technical range: support, networks, cloud, security, development, architecture, data and operations. A typical engagement begins with coverage across common technical disciplines, and then role design where the title does not yet match the work. It is usually a good fit for iT managers hiring into more than one technical discipline. If that does not sound like your situation, use the related services on this page; it is better to change the front door than to force the wrong one.

It is also worth hearing the limits in ordinary language. Support, networks, cloud, security, development, architecture, data and operations are all in scope when we have a brief. If a niche is outside our reach, we will say so. We can run more than one search at a time. A whole-team build still needs priorities, reporting lines and a realistic sequence. We would rather lose a piece of work than invent a promise about coverage, timing, certification or savings.

When you are ready, send a short brief through the quote form with this service selected. Include the location or systems, the timing, and any access, brand or compliance constraint we should not discover later. We will come back with a practical next step, or with a recommendation to start elsewhere. Submitting the form does not create a paid engagement, a booked visit, or a placement.

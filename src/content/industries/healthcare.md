## Plan IT around care, not the other way around

In healthcare and care settings, a failed device or a poor wireless network is a delay in someone else's day. HeyDock provides IT support, on-site dispatch, wireless and security help to UK healthcare organisations, including clinics, care providers and related services, with two rules: we plan around care or clinic hours when you tell us they matter, and we treat patient or service-user information as sensitive.

We will not claim an NHS framework place we do not have. We will not wander a clinical area without an escort if your rules require one.

## How we usually help

Faults and device work are the common start. Wireless in older buildings, Microsoft 365 tenancy hygiene, and careful asset disposal also come up often. Security work starts with the basics: access, email, backups, and a reporting path.

## What we will be careful about

Access is limited. Photographs and walk-throughs follow your site rules. If a task needs a specialist clinical-system vendor, we will not pretend we are that vendor.

Send a brief with the sites, the hours to avoid, and the systems we must not touch without a named owner.

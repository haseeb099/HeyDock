## Keep the site trading, and arrive when the floor can actually receive you

Retail and hospitality IT is local and time-sensitive: a dead payment-adjacent network, a guest Wi-Fi that has collapsed on a Saturday, a back-office PC that prints nothing. HeyDock provides dispatch, wireless, connectivity and support for UK shops, restaurants, hotels and similar sites, with visits planned around opening hours and access.

We are usually not the payment application vendor. We will be honest about that, and still help with the network and devices around it.

## How we usually help

Engineer dispatch and break/fix for devices, wireless for guest and staff separation, connectivity when the incoming service is the weak point, and IT or chat support for chains that need a first line. Multi-site work is a series of confirmed attendances, not a claimed map.

## What we will be careful about

We will not arrive in the middle of service if you have asked us not to. We will not treat a shop unit like an office comms room without looking at it. Landlord and mall rules can delay cabling; we will say so early.

Tell us the trading hours, the site list, and which supplier owns the till or booking platform.

## Work inside the rules you already have, and document what we did

Public sector and publicly funded organisations have approval paths, procurement constraints and a reasonable expectation that work can later be explained. HeyDock can provide consulting, support, security and vendor-selection help in that environment, without claiming a framework place we do not have, and without treating a shortcut as a virtue.

If a request would break a rule you have told us about, we will stop and say so.

## How we usually help

Consulting and vendor selection often come first, because buying is scrutinised. Managed IT and support keep estates operable. Security and compliance support help with controls and evidence, not with certificates we cannot issue.

## What we will be careful about

Access, data handling and change records follow your policies. We will not lobby you to bypass procurement. We will not over-claim experience of a specific agency.

Tell us the approval path, the data classes involved, and whether a competition or a direct award is in play. We will tell you if we are the wrong supplier for the route you must use.

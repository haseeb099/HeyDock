## Treat finance systems as operationally critical, and stay inside your change rules

Finance teams and financial firms feel IT failures immediately: payments, reporting, trading-support tools, or the identity system that gates them. HeyDock provides IT, cloud, continuity and security help to UK finance teams and financial firms, with an explicit line: we are not a regulated financial services provider, and we do not offer financial advice.

What we will do is work inside a written change process, take backups and recovery seriously, and be careful with access.

## How we usually help

Support and managed services keep the day moving. Cloud management and FinOps help once an estate is in a public cloud. Continuity planning and cybersecurity sit close, because an outage or an incident in this sector is rarely 'just IT'. Vendor selection is useful when a new platform is being bought under scrutiny.

## What we will be careful about

We will not sign a regulatory attestation we are not qualified to give. We will not make a production change in the dark to be helpful. If your rules require change records, we will use them.

Tell us the systems that cannot fail quietly, the approval path, and the sites involved. We will tell you if a request is outside IT work.

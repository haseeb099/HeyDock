## Be the brand the client sees; let us be the hands you did not have in that town

Managed service providers already own the client relationship. What they sometimes lack is a person in the right place, or a specialist for a short piece of work. HeyDock provides white-label dispatch, remote support, rollouts, data centre smart hands and related delivery for UK MSPs, under a written scope. The visit is still only booked when city, skills and access are confirmed.

We are not trying to replace your service desk or your brand. We are trying to make a ticket that needs a person on a site finish cleanly.

## How we usually help

Dispatch and break/fix are the most common requests. Remote technical talent can sit inside your tools. Rollouts and colo smart hands appear when a project needs extra bodies. Escalation paths are named so your service desk stays in control.

## What we will be careful about

Client ownership is written down. We will not 'helpfully' suggest they call us next time. If we cannot attend, we will say so rather than half-promise. Reports are written so you can forward or rebrand them as agreed.

Tell us the brand rules, the tool access, and the first site or skill you need. A single visit is a valid start.

## Keep the practice working, and treat client information as the point

Professional firms live on documents, deadlines and trust. A mailbox that is down, a document system that is slow, or a laptop that fails the morning of a meeting is not a minor IT inconvenience. HeyDock supports UK professional services organisations (law, accountancy, consultancy and similar practices) with IT support, managed services, Microsoft 365, security and, where it is justified, software and automation.

We plan visits and changes around the diary you actually have. We do not assume that Thursday afternoon is quiet.

## How we usually help

Most firms need a reliable way to raise faults, a tenant that is not still on default settings, and a sensible approach to phishing and access. Some also need a document or workflow system that is more than a shared drive. The right mix is a brief, not a package we force on you.

If you are an MSP serving this sector, white-label dispatch and support can be agreed so your client still sees your brand.

## What we will be careful about

Client information is the heart of the firm. Access is limited, and disposal of devices is planned rather than informal. We will not claim a sector accreditation we cannot show. We will not schedule a risky change against a filing deadline if you have told us it exists.

Send a brief with the systems you rely on, the sites you occupy, and any constraints around confidentiality or hours. An enquiry is not an engagement.
